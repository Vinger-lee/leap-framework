The model assumes H≥0, g>0, release from rest, gravity alone, negligible drag and approximately constant g, and ends immediately before first impact. Initial height H, accumulated fall distance s and remaining height z are separate. The air-resistance diagram is qualitative and cannot predict a trajectory. The simulator starts at 50m while the original Q1 is fixed at 45m.
